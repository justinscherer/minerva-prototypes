The gap is the headline. Random sampling put pagination impact at ~2% of articles — but that's dominated by short stubs, which make up most of any wiki's article count. Weighted by what people actually read, roughly 1 in 4 page views (mean 24%, up to 1-in-2 on vi/es/pt) would hit a lead section over 300 words. Reader-facing impact of this feature is much larger than raw article-count stats suggest.
